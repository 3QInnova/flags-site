The Flag Guide

Every flag on Flags, what it means, and who can see it — the full 36-flag catalog across all three layers.

This is a different system from the four accountability flags (green, yellow, red, and ghost) described in our Community Guidelines — those are evidence about a specific message or a pattern of behavior. The flags below are self-described traits and signals you choose to put on your own profile, not evidence about anyone. The five flags marked Not user-selectable in the Connection layer are the exception: they're a visual representation of account state (verification, blocks, timeouts) that the accountability system already tracks — shown here for reference, never something you or anyone else can add to your own profile.
